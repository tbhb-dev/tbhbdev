Judge the evidence for ${repo}#${pr}. For a first review, the scope is the pull request's entire change.
Follow the review template. Give one or two sentences of reasons in Summary, in one paragraph of at most 40 words. Record the verdict in draft frontmatter and keep it out of the review body.
Write every finding in the draft's Findings section. Start each bullet with F1: P1 correctness, F2: P2 design, and so on, starting at 1 without gaps. Use up to eight findings, each with one or two sentences and at most 30 words after the kind. Kinds are correctness, security, design, test, and docs. P0 means harm or a security breach, P1 means broken basic use or correctness, P2 means debt worth fixing soon, and P3 means minor. For an inline comment, cite the changed line as `path:line`. Posting moves that finding from the body to an inline comment. Use findings without a location for issues with the whole PR, and cite a check and its result when relevant. P0 and P1 require REQUEST_CHANGES. Omit task checkboxes and restatements of CI gates.
Name the real situation for each finding. End P1 findings with a short `Occurs when:` clause within the word limit. Ground findings in the system's actual committers, hosts, repositories, tool inputs, and callers. Skip cases at every severity when they require an encoding the tools never emit, a hostile user the system never serves, a platform it never runs on, future scale, or a caller that does not exist.
Flag code in the diff that handles an impossible case or adds an option, layer, or abstraction with one user. Ask the author to delete it.
